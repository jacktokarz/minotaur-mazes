How to play

  1. Move Theseus through the maze toward a portal.
  2. After each move, every Minotaur pursues Theseus.
  3. Each Minotaur attempts horizontal movement first, then vertical movement, making up to two moves per turn.
  4. Walls block movement. Reach any portal without being caught.
