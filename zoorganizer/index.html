How to play

You're opening your own zoo. You've painstakingly set up elaborate environments for your animals to enjoy, and they've finally arrived today. Place your animals in the environments that make them all happy.

  1. Every animal must be placed in an enclosure. Each enclosure is labelled with its environment type.
  2. An enclosure can hold 0 - 3 animals.
  3. Two enclosures are "next door" if they share a fence.
  4. Drag an animal from New Arrivals into an enclosure. You can move it to another enclosure, or drag it back to New Arrivals, at any time.
  5. There is exactly one correct arrangement. When every animal is placed, press Submit.
  6. If your arrangement is wrong, you won't be told why. You'll have to reset the board and start over, so think it through!
