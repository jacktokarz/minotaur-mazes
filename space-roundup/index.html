How to play

  1. You're a round little space creature, and your friends are scattered around the asteroid field.
  2. Pick a direction and you'll fly as far as you can, until you hit an asteroid or a friend.
  3. Fly into a friend to pick them up. You stop right in front of them, and they come along with you.
  4. The exit stays locked (and stops you like an asteroid) until you've found every friend. Then fly into it to escape!
  5. You can move as many times as you like, and reset whenever you get stuck.
