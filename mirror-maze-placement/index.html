Mirror Maze (Placement)

Bounce the light so every letter finds its match.

How to play

  1. Each letter on the edge of the maze shines a beam of light straight into the maze.
  2. Place mirrors so that every letter is connected by light to all the other copies of the same letter, and never to a different letter.
  3. Mirrors are double-sided and turn light 90°. Light travels in straight lines through hallways, and walls stop it.
  4. Light stops at the first letter it reaches, and that letter must match. A letter inside the maze can be reached from any side.
  5. Click or tap a hallway square to place a mirror. Click again to turn it the other way, and a third time to remove it.
  6. When you think every letter is connected, press Check to shine the light and see where it goes.
