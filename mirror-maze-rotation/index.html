How to play

  1. Each matching pair of icons on the edge of the maze needs to be connected by light. The pairs fire one at a time: the light shoots from one icon and has to reach its partner.
  2. The mirrors are already in place. They're double-sided and turn light 90°. Light travels in straight lines, walls stop it, and it stops at the first icon it reaches, which must match. If an icon has a match in the middle of the maze, both outer icons fire together, and both beams must reach a match.
  3. At the end of each pair's turn, every mirror its light bounced off flips to the other diagonal (/ becomes \, and \ becomes /). Mirrors don't move while the light is travelling, so a mirror hit twice in one turn is in the same position both times, and still only flips once.
  4. Your job: choose the order the pairs fire in, so that every pair's light reaches its partner.
  5. Click or tap a pair's icon to give it the next number. Click it again to take the number away.
  6. When every pair has a number, press Check. The first pair fires; press Continue to watch its mirrors turn, and Continue again to fire the next pair.
  7. If a pair misses, its number and every number after it are cleared, and the mirrors go back to how they started.
